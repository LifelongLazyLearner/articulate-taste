# Taste profile — Mira

## Core Principles

### verified-or-it-does-not-go-out — core

**Statement.** Nothing goes out that I have not checked myself.

**Boundary.** It binds what I certify, not what everyone else certifies.

**Test.** Do I know this, or did someone tell me?

### unfollowable-is-unusable — core

**Statement.** A number I cannot retrace is not a result.

**Boundary.** This is about anything load-bearing.

**Confirmed by.** [2026-08-07-2019-spreadsheet] — the profile predicted this judgment correctly.

### label-the-guess — provisional

**Statement.** A guess said in the voice of a finding is worse than silence.

**Boundary.** It does not license keeping quiet.

### ignored-over-implicated — provisional

**Statement.** I would rather be ignored than be the reason something failed.

**Boundary.** It stops where staying quiet is itself the unchecked thing.
